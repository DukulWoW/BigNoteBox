# BigNoteBox v1.9.0

## New
- Language selector in Settings > General and on the first page of the setup wizard: choose English or Simplified Chinese regardless of your WoW client's language (retail only for now)
- Chinese font set: with Chinese active, the font pickers show Chinese-capable fonts (WoW Hei and WoW Kai) instead of the Latin ones, and your English and Chinese font choices are remembered separately
- Support for font packs: optional add-ons that bring extra fonts. The first one, BigNoteBox Fonts CN, adds six Chinese fonts. Settings > General shows which packs are installed, and a missing pack's icon copies its download link

## Fixed
- The font picker showed 9 boxes instead of 8 since WoW's default font was added. It's back to a clean 8-font grid, with WoW's default font as its own checkbox below it
- The sticky note settings and the New Note dialog showed every LibSharedMedia font as a card. They are now in a dropdown below the font grid, as in Settings > Appearance
- Sticky note titles in Chinese or Korean showed as empty boxes
- Chinese clients: deleting a tag caused an error
- The Chinese translation is now complete, including the migration windows
