# BigNoteBox v2.1.0

## All versions

### New

#### Languages and spelling

- German translation: BigNoteBox speaks Deutsch, the Help book and Features tab included. It follows a German game client, or pick it in Settings > General > Language.
- Spelling in French and German: install the new BigNoteBox Spelling Pack and pick the language in Settings > Modules > Spelling.
- German spelling understands compound words (Gildenbank, Questgeber), checks every noun with its capital, and a long misspelled word gets suggestions for the broken part (Drachenschuppenrüstung). Settings > Modules > Spelling can limit it to the compounds the German dictionary allows.
- Each note can have its own spelling language: right-click a marked word, then Language, or use the new language button on the formatting toolbar. A note checked in another language than your game's shows that language's shield next to its title.

#### Notes

- Move your notes over from SimpleNotepad and Siggis Notepad (Siggis Notepad's trashed notes go to the Trash).
- Vendor notes: a new button on the vendor window saves everything the vendor sells (with prices and limited stock) in its NPC note and Reference Box, tagged Vendor. Click again on a later visit to update the list.

### Change

#### Spelling

- Spelling page: pick the language by clicking its flag (the dropdown is gone); every installed dictionary's flag is in color.
- A note's hover tooltip shows one language line for each script in it (Korean, Chinese, Russian...).

#### Notes and editing

- Quest notes: the quest is a link heading at the top (right-click it to copy the Wowhead address), Objectives and Rewards get their own headings and dividers. Every rich capture note (quests, books, letters, dialogue) ends on a Notes part for your own words. From Dialogue UI too: its quest and rewards become game links with their icons, money shows as coins and XP with its icon.
- Right-click a quest, item, spell or currency link in a note: Copy Wowhead URL.
- Undo: the Forced interval slider now goes down to 0.5 seconds.

#### Settings

- Settings > Backup: a line under the import box says that a large backup can make the game seem frozen for a moment.
- Settings > Modules > Tags: the tag tree settings and where the Tag Manager opens have their own page now (they were on the Notes tab).
- Buttons fit their labels in every language: a long label gets a little smaller, and if it still does not fit it ends in ... with the whole text on hover.

### Fixed

- Rich notes: drop shadows and the note's own font (Note Settings > Appearance) now show in the rich view. A sticky's rich view follows its Sticky settings font and outline, like a plain sticky.
